# Blinkit Power BI Dashboard Project
![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/26de50d5-5274-433e-8424-6d7fff84aed3)

## Project Statement

Blinkit, a fast-paced quick-commerce platform, delivers groceries and essentials in minutes. This project involves building a dynamic Power BI dashboard to help Blinkit monitor KPIs, optimize operations, and make informed business decisions.

---

## Project Goals

- Visualize key metrics: orders, delivery time, inventory, feedback, and profitability.
- Enable real-time data interaction and filtering.
- Provide actionable insights for business efficiency and customer satisfaction.

---

## Target Audience

- Business Stakeholders: High-level performance summaries.
- Operations Managers: Data for process optimization.
- Data Analysts: Deeper backend insights.

---

## Key Features

- **Dynamic Charts**: Bar, Line, Pie, Donut, and Heat Maps.
- **Slicers & Filters**: By product, time period, region, and channel.
- **Drill-Down Capabilities**: Detailed analysis on click.
- **Export Options**: Easily share dashboards as reports.

---

## Dashboard Pages Overview

### Sales Overview

- **Revenue by Product Category**: Top-selling items with dynamic slicer (Top 5/10/20/50/100).
- **Monthly Sales Trends**: YoY performance (2023–2025) and growth%.
- **Payment & Customer Segment**: Usage and segment balance via donut charts.

### Customer Page

- **Customer Metrics**: Total, New, Lost, and Repeat Customers (Cards).
- **Customer Growth Trends**: Monthly trends and performance dips.
- **Feedback Sentiment**: Positive (70.5%), Neutral (19.5%), Negative (9.9%).

### Inventory Page

- **Category-wise Stock**: Availability, damaged stock, and sales volume.
- **Stock Quality Overview**: Gauge charts for usable vs damaged stock.

### Marketing Page

- **Acquisition Channels**: App, Email, SMS, and Social Media share.
- **Annual Marketing Summary**: Impressions, revenue, clicks, conversions, spend & ROAS.
---

- Improved visibility into operations and customer behavior.
